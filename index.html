How it works

Your records, compared. With receipts.

1

Log the way you talk

One sentence can become several records: hours of sleep, a mood, an expense. Nothing is saved until you confirm. Apple Health can fill in the rest.

2

It finds links, with the days behind them

On the lowest-mood days, daily spending ran $113 higher: 17 such days against 64 others. Every link comes in your own units, as a hypothesis, not a verdict.

DEMO DATA
3

It checks itself

It asks one question to rule out another cause, keeps every claim on the record and counts how often its predictions came true. Any link can become a 20-day check: ten days with, ten without, drawn at random.

Claims on the record

Called 8 of 10. Chance alone: about 28%.

Before it has enough days, it says nothing at all. After that, every claim stays on the record with its sample size and standing, and you can replay the search that found it.

What's inside

One record for the whole day

Body map

Tap where it feels off: sharp, dull, strong. Add blood pressure, resting pulse or HRV right there.

Food, offline

7,973 foods and 2.8 million barcodes in a database on the phone. Or describe the meal in words and get an estimate.

Money, typed by hand

No bank login. Subscriptions and rent wait for your confirmation before they count.

A journal that notices

The app reads your journal on the phone and offers turns of thought from your own entries as hypotheses.

Practices without points

54 practices with instructions. A skill moves through six positions, from unseen to passing on. No XP, no badges.

Labs and the doctor

Lab results against their ranges, and questions to bring to your doctor. No diagnosis, no advice.

Life in chapters

Moves, jobs, relationships, losses: the weeks after are read against the weeks before, and a change is named only when it beats chance.

A library for bad days

23 public-domain manuals, from field medicine to bread and soap, answered by the AI on the phone. After a one-time model download, it works in airplane mode too.

Ask your own records

Bibliotheca, the in-app AI, runs on Apple Intelligence on the device. It does not flatter or invent.

Privacy

Local-first by design

Your records are stored on your iPhone. There is no account, no server sync and no analytics.

  • The AI runs on the device by default. Private Cloud Compute stays off until you turn it on.
  • Lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
  • Export to Markdown, JSON or a full copy whenever you like.
  • Delete all data from Privacy & Data whenever you are done.
